One Trip

One trip that I remember was when you and I went to Smithfield. This trip is important because it was something simple. Usually, with elaborately planned trips like when we traveled the West Coast or went to Alaska, there's a significant amount of effort put into them to make sure that they are fun and enjoyable. With Smithfield, we were able to venture off on a day trip with no previous plans and still have a good time, which is something that I value!
